Delete your Swappr account

Swappr by Mikoshi Gear · package com.tangm.swappr

You can delete your Swappr account and the data attached to it at any time. There is no subscription or payment record to cancel — Swappr is free.

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

1Open Swappr and go to the Profile tab.
2Tap Settings (gear icon).
3Tap Delete Account and confirm.
4You are signed out and your data is removed immediately.

Option 2 — by email

If you cannot open the app, email admin@mikoshigear.ca with the subject “Delete my Swappr account”, from the email address on the account. We reply to confirm and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: email address, username, display name, bio and profile photo.
  • All your listings and their photos.
  • Offers and trade records you are part of, and the messages in them — the other person loses the conversation too.
  • The friend-discovery hash, if you had turned discovery on.
  • Notification tokens and blocked-user entries you created.

Your listings, messages and profile are removed immediately. Image files (listing photos, avatars, chat images) are deleted from our storage by a daily cleanup — within 24 hours.

What we keep, and why

  • Backups — deleted content can persist in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being purged. Nothing in a backup is visible in the app.
  • We do not keep a shadow profile after deletion: reports, reviews, blocks and trades involving you are removed along with the account.
Heads up: deletion is permanent. If you sign up again later you start with an empty account and a new username history.
