Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Applies to the Swappr app (com.tangm.swappr) and this website

Swappr is a friends-only swapping app: people trade items with people they already know, and no money changes hands. Because the app only works between friends, the data we hold is deliberately small. This page explains exactly what that is.

1. What we collect

  • Account. Your email address, a username, and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider) or your Google account identifier if you sign in with Google. Optionally a profile photo, display name and short bio.
  • Listings. The title, description, condition, category and photos of items you post.
  • Approximate location. Items carry a coarse location so the app can show distance (for example “0.8 km away”). We do not show or store your street address.
  • Offers and messages. The offers you make or receive and the messages you exchange inside a trade. Both people in a trade can read them.
  • Safety data. Reports and blocks you submit, so we can review them.
  • Friend discovery (optional, off by default). If you turn on “Let friends find you”, your device computes a one-way SHA-256 hash of your own email address and phone number and sends only those hashes. Your address book never leaves your phone — no names, no numbers, no raw contacts. Turning the switch off deletes the hash from our servers.
  • Notifications. Where you enable them, a device push token so we can deliver trade updates.
  • Product events (you can turn this off). Coarse, first-party usage events — app opened, an item viewed, an offer sent, a message sent, a trade completed — recorded with your account id and the relevant item or trade id. No message text, no listing text, no device fingerprint. Turn it off any time under Settings → Share Usage Data; the next event is not recorded.
What we do not do: no advertising SDKs, no third-party tracking, no selling or renting personal data, no precise GPS tracking, no background access to your contacts.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your feed and trades, deliver messages.
  • To connect you with friends — only when you have opted in to discovery.
  • To keep people safe: reviewing reports, enforcing blocks, removing abusive content.
  • To send you notifications about offers, accepts and completions you have asked for.
  • To fix problems: diagnosing errors and keeping the service available.
  • To understand which features are actually used — in aggregate, never to profile you or to target advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We use service providers that process data on our behalf, under contract and only for the purposes above:

  • Supabase — database, file storage and authentication, hosted in Canada (ca-central-1).
  • Google — only if you choose “Sign in with Google”, to verify your identity.
  • Our email and push delivery providers — to send sign-in and notification messages.

We also disclose data where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to protect someone’s safety. We do not sell your data to anyone.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, listings, messages, reports: until you delete your account.
  • Image files: removed with your listings, and any unreferenced files are purged by a daily cleanup (within 24 hours).
  • Product events and crash diagnostics: deleted with your account.
  • Backups: purged within 30 days of deletion.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data at any time: in the app go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or use our public deletion page. No email to us is required.
  • Withdraw friend discovery at any time with the same switch that turned it on; the stored hash is deleted immediately.
  • Access, correct or export your data — email us and we will respond within 30 days.
  • Notifications can be turned off in your device settings.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted row-by-row, so you can only read your own profile and the items and trades you are part of. Passwords are stored as hashes, never as plain text.

7. Children

Swappr is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to use it, and old enough to enter a contract where you live.

8. Where your data lives

Our database and file storage are hosted in Canada. Our providers may access data from the United States to operate and support the service, under standard contractual protections.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.

10. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: admin@mikoshigear.ca. Swappr is operated by Mikoshi Gear, Toronto, Ontario, Canada. If you are in Canada, you may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
